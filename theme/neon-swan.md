# Neon Swan

A dramatic dark theme with glowing aquamarine and royal purple neon accents over pure black, drawn from the Swan Moon portfolio site — elegant script headings paired with airy geometric body text.

## Color Palette

- **Pure Black**: `#000000` - Page and panel background base
- **Aquamarine**: `#7FFFD4` - Headings, icons, and primary neon glow
- **Rebecca Purple**: `#663399` - Links, accents, and secondary neon glow
- **Light Sea Green**: `#20B2AA` - Hover/visited states and soft text shadows
- **Twilight Slate**: `#494762` - Active/hover surface fills
- **Light Gray**: `#D3D3D3` - Body text on black
- **White**: `#FFFFFF` - High-emphasis interactive text

### Micro-Accents (minimal use only)

- **Magenta**: `#FF00FF` - Tiny elements: unordered-list bullets, markers, tick dots, the interpunct/middot separators inside eyebrow lines; also visited-link text
- **Dodger Blue**: `#1E90FF` - Tiny elements: dashes, dividers between inline items, sub-list bullets; also the letter-spaced eyebrow/subtitle line under script headings

Micro-accents must never be used for headings, body text, buttons, backgrounds, or any large fill — they exist to add a rare fleck of contrast at the smallest scale (a bullet, a dash, a dot).

## Typography

- **Headers**: Sacramento (flowing script, thin weight) with aquamarine fill and layered purple + aquamarine text-shadow glow
- **Body Text**: Poppins Light (300) / Century Gothic, fallback 'URW Gothic L', sans-serif

## Signature Effects

- **Dual neon glow**: paired box-shadows `3px 3px 20px #663399, 5px 5px 20px #7FFFD4` on containers and dividers
- **Text glow**: `3px 3px 5px #663399, 3.5px 3.5px 5px #7FFFD4` on script headings; `2px 2px 2px #20B2AA` on subheadings
- **Two-tone borders**: hairline (`.1px`) borders — purple on top/left, aquamarine on bottom/right
- **Circular imagery**: images and avatars use `border-radius: 50%`
- **Italic purple callouts**: pull-quotes in italic Rebecca Purple with a 1px aquamarine shadow
- **Links**: unvisited Dodger Blue `#1E90FF`, visited Magenta `#FF00FF`, hover Light Sea Green `#20B2AA` — no glow/text-shadow on links, it reduces legibility

## Best Used For

Personal portfolios, creative resumes, digital marketing brands, nightlife or music promos, crypto/tech personal brands, anything wanting an elegant neon-on-black identity.
