CCU Brand Guidelines

Crypto Credit Union · Visual Identity v1.0

Brand Overview

CCU — Crypto Credit Union — is a digital-first financial brand. The visual identity is neon on black: an icy electric blue and cyan glow over a pure black canvas, inspired by circuit boards and coin tokens. The look should always feel luminous, precise, and airy — never cluttered or washed out.

“Luminous, precise, airy.” — the three words every CCU layout should pass.

Logo

The primary logo is the circular circuit-ring mark with the CCU wordmark and coin icons. The extracted CCU wordmark is the secondary mark, used in the round coin badge at page footers and wherever the full ring is too detailed.

  • Always place the logo on black · its own background is part of the mark
  • Keep clear space around the ring equal to the height of the CCU letters
  • The wordmark badge is a 64px black circle, blue stroke, cyan glow — box-shadow: 0 0 20px #00FFFF
  • Use the primary logo as the favicon and header mark; the badge closes the page

Do

  • Round the logo with border-radius: 50%
  • Scale proportionally, 150–180px in headers
  • Let it glow only with theme shadows

Don't

  • Recolor, stretch, or rotate the mark
  • Place it on white or colored fills
  • Crop the ring except for the approved wordmark

  • Accents stay at fleck scale — bullets, dashes, links & tiny elements only; never fills, text blocks, or buttons
  • Black is the only background · color arrives through glow, never fill
  • Token greens and blues (USDT · USDC) appear only as the 30px corner coins

Signature Effects

  • Dual neon glow — box-shadow: 1.5px 1.5px 30px #00FFFF, 2.5px 2.5px 30px #11B4F5
  • Heading glow — text-shadow: 1.5px 1.5px 5px #00FFFF, 1.75px 1.75px 5px #11B4F5
  • Title glow — text-shadow: 0 0 6px #00FFFF
  • Soft link glow — text-shadow: 0.5px 0.5px 2px #00FFFF
  • Icon glow — box-shadow: 0 0 20px #00FFFF
  • Border glows are stronger than text glows — containers radiate, letters whisper

Components

  • Buttons are pill-shaped — border-radius: 999px, 4px light gray border, box-shadow: 0 0 8px #00FFFF, content-sized with 8px/16px padding
  • Panels are two-tone — hairline borders, cyan top/left, blue bottom/right
  • Imagery is circular — border-radius: 50% on avatars and photos
  • Dividers take the dual neon glow
  • USDT sits bottom-left, USDC bottom-right, 30×30px, inside the glowing panel
