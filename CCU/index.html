Neon Swan

CCU Edition · Theme Preview · Swan Moon Portfolio

Color Palette

Primary — aquamarine & royal blue on a navy canvas

Highlight accents — minimal use: bullets, dashes, links & other tiny elements only

Typography & Elements

Subheading — Century Gothic / Poppins with soft royal blue glow

Body text is Poppins Light over navy in light gray, airy and readable. Links look like this unvisited link in white or this visited link in pale yellow, and pull-quotes take the italic blue treatment:

“Content is King.” — Bill Gates
  • Unordered lists take a tiny white dot bullet — white otherwise appears only on unvisited links
  • Inline separators and dashes render in pale yellow
  • Nested items use a pale yellow em-dash marker
    • Both accents stay at fleck scale — never fills, text, or buttons

Active / Hover State

Two-tone panel — hairline borders run royal blue on the top/left and aquamarine on the bottom/right, echoed by the dual neon box-glow on containers and dividers.

Signature Effects

  • Dual neon glow — box-shadow: 3px 3px 20px #4169E1, 5px 5px 20px #7FFFD4
  • Heading glow — text-shadow: 3px 3px 5px #4169E1, 3.5px 3.5px 5px #7FFFD4
  • Soft link glow — text-shadow: 2px 2px 2px #4169E1
  • Highlight accents — white #FFFFFF bullets · pale yellow #FFFFE0 dashes — tiny elements only
  • Circular imagery — border-radius: 50% on avatars and photos
  • Icon glow — black circle, aquamarine stroke, box-shadow: 0 0 14px #4169E1
  • Headers — Sacramento script, aquamarine
  • Body — Poppins 300 / Century Gothic, light gray
