Color Palette

Primary — vaporwave cyan & purple on a charcoal canvas

Highlight accents — minimal use: bullets, dashes, links & other tiny elements only

Typography & Elements

Subheading — Century Gothic / Poppins with soft purple glow

Body text is Poppins Light over charcoal in light gray, airy and readable. Links look like this unvisited link in cyan or this visited link in hot pink (hovering turns either mint), and pull-quotes take the italic purple treatment:

“Content is King.” — Bill Gates
  • Unordered lists take a tiny hot pink dot bullet — pink otherwise appears only on visited links
  • Inline dashes render in pale yellow, separators · in mint
  • Nested items use a mint em-dash marker
    • All accents stay at fleck scale — never fills, text, or buttons

Active / Hover State

Two-tone panel — hairline borders run purple on the top/left and cyan on the bottom/right, echoed by the dual neon box-glow on containers and dividers.

Signature Effects

  • Dual neon glow — box-shadow: 1.5px 1.5px 20px #B967FF, 2.5px 2.5px 20px #01CDFE
  • Heading glow — text-shadow: 1.5px 1.5px 5px #B967FF, 1.75px 1.75px 5px #01CDFE
  • Soft link glow — text-shadow: 1.5px 1.5px 2px #B967FF
  • Highlight accents — hot pink #FF71CE bullets · pale yellow #FFFB96 dashes · mint #05FFA1 separators & hovers — tiny elements only
  • Circular imagery — border-radius: 50% on avatars and photos
  • Icon glow — black circle, cyan stroke, box-shadow: 0 0 14px #B967FF
  • Headers — Sacramento script, cyan
  • Body — Poppins 300 / Century Gothic, light gray
