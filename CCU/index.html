Color Palette

Primary — aqua & white on a charcoal canvas

Highlight accents — minimal use: bullets, dashes, links & other tiny elements only

Typography & Elements

Subheading — Century Gothic / Poppins with soft white glow

Body text is Poppins Light over charcoal in light gray, airy and readable. Links look like this unvisited link in dodger blue or this visited link in magenta (hovering turns either white), and pull-quotes take the italic white treatment:

“Content is King.” — Bill Gates
  • Unordered lists take a tiny dodger blue dot bullet — magenta appears only on visited links
  • Inline dashes render in pale yellow, separators · in white
  • Nested items use a white em-dash marker
    • Both accents stay at fleck scale — never fills, text, or buttons

Active / Hover State

Two-tone panel — hairline borders run white on the top/left and aqua on the bottom/right, echoed by the dual neon box-glow on containers and dividers.

Signature Effects

  • Dual neon glow — box-shadow: 1.5px 1.5px 20px #FFFFFF, 2.5px 2.5px 20px #00FFFF
  • Heading glow — text-shadow: 1.5px 1.5px 5px #FFFFFF, 1.75px 1.75px 5px #00FFFF
  • Soft link glow — text-shadow: 1.5px 1.5px 2px #FFFFFF
  • Highlight accents — dodger blue #1E90FF bullets · pale yellow #EEE8AA dashes · magenta #FF00FF visited links · white #FFFFFF separators & hovers — tiny elements only
  • Circular imagery — border-radius: 50% on avatars and photos
  • Icon glow — black circle, aqua stroke, box-shadow: 0 0 14px #FFFFFF
  • Headers — Sacramento script, aqua
  • Body — Poppins 300 / Century Gothic, light gray
